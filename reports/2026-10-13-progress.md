### What was done

**XcodeGen setup**
- Installed Homebrew + XcodeGen on Mac
- Wrote `project.yml` at repo root — generates `Ledger.xcodeproj` with a single `xcodegen` command
- Eliminates the manual 13-step Mac_Setup.md process entirely

**Fixed all iOS 16 vs 17 compatibility errors (17 files)**

- Changed all `onChange(of:) { _, new in }` two-argument closures to single-argument form
- Wrapped all `ContentUnavailableView` usages with `if #available(iOS 17, *)`

**Fixed missing CoreData properties**
- Added `splitsJSON` to CDTransaction+CoreDataProperties.swift
- Added `thesis` and `targetPrice` to WatchlistItem+CoreDataProperties.swift
- (Both were in the .xcdatamodeld model but missing from the generated Swift files)

**Fixed other compile errors**
