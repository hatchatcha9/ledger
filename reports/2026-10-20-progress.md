## What was done

### iOS 16 compatibility fixes (Windows → GitHub)

Audited the entire codebase for iOS 17-only APIs and fixed all found instances:

| File | Issue | Fix |
|------|-------|-----|
| `ChatView.swift` | Two-arg `onChange` closure (iOS 17 only) | Changed to single-arg form |
| `DashboardView.swift` | `.symbolEffect(.pulse)` (iOS 17 only) | Wrapped in `if #available(iOS 17, *)` |
| `PortfolioView.swift` | `SectorMark` donut chart (iOS 17 only) | Wrapped in `if #available(iOS 17, *)`, legend shows on iOS 16 |
| `LedgerWidget.swift` | `.containerBackground(for: .widget)` (iOS 17 only) | Added `widgetBackground()` helper with `.background()` fallback |
| `AddEditPriceAlertView.swift` | `requestAuthorization` closure missing second arg | Fixed `{ _ in }` → `{ _, _ in }` |
| `ChatView.swift` | `.inlinesOnlyPreservingWhitespace` (iOS 16.4+) | Dropped options param, use default `AttributedString(markdown:)` |
| `project.yml` | Global deployment target was 17.0, conflicting with target's 16.0 | Unified both to 16.0 |

Also confirmed `.contentTransition(.numericText())` (used in 13 places) is iOS 16+ — no fix needed.
