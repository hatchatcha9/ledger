Privacy Policy — Ledger

Last updated: October 2026

What data Ledger collects

Nothing. Ledger stores all your financial data locally on your device using Apple's CoreData framework. No account, sign-in, or internet connection is required to use the app.

What data leaves your device

Two optional, on-demand features contact external servers:

  • Live prices — Ledger fetches asset prices from Yahoo Finance. Your asset symbols are sent as part of the request URL. No personal data is included.
  • Ask Claude / Market Intelligence — When you tap "Ask Claude" or open an asset's Market Intelligence card, a summary of your anonymised financial data or recent social posts is sent to a proxy server to generate an AI response. No names, account numbers, or identifying information are included. The proxy does not log or store request content beyond the current request.

Both features are entirely optional. Ledger works fully offline without them.

Third-party services

  • Yahoo Finance API — price data only; see Yahoo Privacy Policy.
  • StockTwits API — public social sentiment; no authentication required; see StockTwits Privacy Policy.
  • Anthropic Claude — AI analysis via a self-hosted proxy; see Anthropic Privacy Policy.

Data retention

All data lives only on your device. Deleting the app removes all data permanently.

Children

Ledger is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from them.
