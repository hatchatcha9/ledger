Privacy Policy

Ledger: Net Worth & Portfolio  ·  Effective October 2026

Short version: Ledger stores your financial data on your device and in your personal iCloud account. We do not collect, sell, or share your financial information with anyone.

Data We Collect

Ledger does not collect personal data. All information you enter — accounts, transactions, portfolio holdings, journal entries, and budgets — is stored exclusively on your device using CoreData and synced to your personal iCloud account via CloudKit if you have iCloud enabled.

iCloud Sync

If you have iCloud enabled, your data syncs across your own devices via Apple's CloudKit service. This data is stored in your private iCloud container and is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. We cannot access it.

Ask Claude (AI Chat)

When you use the Ask Claude feature, a summary of your financial data (account balances, monthly cash flow totals, and portfolio value — no transaction details or account names unless you include them in your message) is sent to our AI relay service, which forwards it to Anthropic's Claude API to generate a response. This data is:

  • Transmitted over HTTPS
  • Not stored by us beyond the duration of the request
  • Subject to Anthropic's Privacy Policy

You can disable this feature at any time by not using the Ask Claude button. No AI features run in the background.

Live Market Prices

Portfolio and watchlist prices are fetched from Yahoo Finance's public quote API. Your device sends ticker symbols (e.g. "AAPL", "BTC-USD") to retrieve prices. No personal information is included in these requests.

Analytics & Tracking

Ledger does not use any analytics SDKs, advertising frameworks, or third-party tracking. We do not track you across apps or websites.

Data Deletion

You can delete all your data at any time by deleting the app from your device. To remove iCloud data, go to iPhone Settings → [Your Name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → Ledger → Delete Data.

Children

Ledger is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update the effective date above. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance.
